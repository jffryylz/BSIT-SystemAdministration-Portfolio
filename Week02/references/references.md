## Note on Source Selection

Official primary sources were preferred throughout. Certification details were taken from the certifying body's own page rather than from training vendors or aggregator sites, because certification names, exam codes and retirement dates change and third-party pages are frequently out of date. Two examples encountered while preparing this project:

- **AWS Certified SysOps Administrator – Associate** has been renamed **AWS Certified CloudOps Engineer – Associate (SOA-C03)**. Several third-party sites still list the former name.
- The **Oracle VirtualBox Extension Pack** is licensed separately from the base package and is not free for business use, a condition that is easy to miss if only the download page is consulted.

Internet service provider information reflects publicly published business offerings at the time of writing. No provider was contacted and no service was purchased for this academic project; plan tiers and pricing must be confirmed directly with the provider before any real procurement decision.
